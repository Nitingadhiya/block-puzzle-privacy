Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information Cozy Blocks ("the game", "we") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by Krishna.

Summary

  • The game has no accounts and we don't run our own servers.
  • We use Google services for ads, analytics, crash reports and purchases. They collect device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • Your progress (coins, levels, scenes) is stored only on your device.

Information collected

Stored on your device only. Your game progress, settings, coins, boosters and purchase status are saved on your device and never sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Reminders. If you allow notifications, the daily reminder is scheduled on your device. No data is sent anywhere to do this, and you can turn it off in the game's settings or your device settings.

Collected by third-party services we use:

AdMobGoogleAdvertising ID, device and app information, approximate location from IP address, ad interactionsTo show ads, measure them and prevent fraud

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Ads and your choices

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, and wherever else the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Buying Remove ads turns off ads between games. Rewarded ads, which you choose to watch for a reward, stay available.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept by Google for 14 months. Crash reports are kept for 90 days. The game has no accounts, so this data is not linked to your name. To ask about deleting your data, email us with your device model and the approximate dates you played, and we will tell you what can be removed and help with the request.

Security

All information sent by the services above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be noted in the game's release notes.
